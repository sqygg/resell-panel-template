Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ deploy_sha[:7] if deploy_sha else '2026' }}

1. Who we are

SkriptX Panel ("we", "us") is a reselling platform that lets approved resellers purchase gaming product keys at a discount and deliver them to their customers.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data: email, display name, Discord handle, and (for applicants) website — provided when you apply or are approved.
  • Order data: products purchased, amounts, quantities, and delivery status. We do not store the delivered key values after delivery.
  • Linked accounts: if you connect SellAuth, we store the linked customer email to mirror your balance; if you link Discord (OAuth), we store your Discord user ID and username to send you 2FA and delivery notifications.
  • Technical data: IP address (for rate limiting and abuse prevention), and basic usage/audit logs.

3. Cookies

We use a single essential session cookie to keep you signed in. It is marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict so it cannot be read by scripts. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can decline the cookie notice, but you will need to sign in again on each visit.

4. How we use data

  • To provide the service: authenticate you, process orders, deliver keys, and apply your discount.
  • To secure the platform: rate-limit abuse, prevent fraud, and notify you of sensitive actions.
  • To communicate: send 2FA codes and delivery alerts to your linked Discord, if enabled.

5. Sharing

We share data only with our payment/stock provider (SellAuth) as needed to fulfil orders. We do not sell your data to third parties.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by contacting the store owner. API keys are hashed and shown only once; revoke them immediately if you suspect exposure.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach the owner via the Discord server linked on the home page.
